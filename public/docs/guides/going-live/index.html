Going-live checklist

Project and keys

Section titled “Project and keys”
  • A production project exists, separate from sandbox, with its own API key in your secret manager.
  • The key is only in server environment variables and in the widget embed; it is not in source control, logs or mobile app bundles beyond what the widget needs.
  • You know how to rotate the key (dashboard, or POST /v1/projects/{id}/api-keys/rotate) and who is allowed to.
  • Platform API calls use a platform key (psk_…) held only on servers, one key per service, and you know how to revoke one in the dashboard.

Origins and passkeys

Section titled “Origins and passkeys”
  • Every production origin that renders the widget, <RiftAuth> or <RiftApprove> is in allowed origins.
  • If you run passkey ceremonies on your own domain, its RP ID is in the project’s allowed RP IDs.
  • Onboarding asks for a second passkey and the security screen lists passkeys, warns at one and blocks removing the last.
  • Customers who skip passkey setup are routed back to it before any money action (409 PASSKEY_SETUP_REQUIRED).

Money movement

Section titled “Money movement”
  • Every fund-moving call handles 202 APPROVAL_REQUIRED, stores approvalId and shows the approval to the customer.
  • Nothing retries money calls automatically; SDK retries: 0 on servers. Timeouts trigger a status lookup, not a resend.
  • Payouts validate the beneficiary and show the account name before the customer approves.
  • Your payment states map Rift statuses (processing, completed, refunding, refunded, expired, cancelled, failed), and compliance holds never prompt a second payment.
  • Amount limits per corridor are enforced in your UI (payment corridors & limits).
  • Gas: you know who pays for which operations and what happens when a free allowance or sponsor cap is reached.

Reconciliation and events

Section titled “Reconciliation and events”
  • A daily job reconciles your records against the ledger (GET /v1/platform/ledger, Beta) or per-customer ledgers.
  • Webhook endpoints (Beta) verify Rift-Signature on the raw body, reject stale timestamps and de-duplicate on event id.
  • You log X-Request-Id / requestId for every failed call.

Data and compliance

Section titled “Data and compliance”
  • Consent for statements or credit scoring is captured in your product and recorded with the consents API before you use the data.
  • Revocation is wired: withdrawing consent deletes the consent record and your next jobs exclude the customer.
  • KYC and sanctions requirements for your licence are configured with Rift for the production project.
  • Your terms explain that the customer’s passkeys are the only way to move their funds, and what that means if all are lost.

Launch

Section titled “Launch”
  • One small real transfer, payout and (if enabled) onramp end to end in production, approved with a real passkey.
  • Support knows where to find the approval, ledger entry and order for a customer complaint.
  • You have a Rift contact for incidents. See pricing & sales.
