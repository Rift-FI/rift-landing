Rift Accounts

Beta

Rift Accounts are in beta. Open accounts and test small amounts first; tell us before you move volume.

A Rift Account is a local-currency account that belongs to your project. You collect money into it from mobile money, keep a balance, and pay out from it to mobile money, banks, tills and paybills. Each project gets one account per currency:

Currency Country Collect from Pay out to
KES Kenya M-Pesa (Safaricom), Airtel Mobile money, bank, till, paybill
TZS Tanzania Vodacom, Halotel, Airtel, Yas / Tigo Mobile money
GHS Ghana MTN, AirtelTigo, Telecel Mobile money
UGX Uganda MTN, Airtel Mobile money
MWK Malawi Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba Mobile money

How your money stays separate

Section titled “How your money stays separate”

The money itself sits in Rift’s settlement wallet for each currency, held with our licensed payment partner. Rift runs a double-entry ledger on top of it, and every account has its own balance in that ledger:

  • Every movement is written as balanced entries in the ledger. Money is never created or lost between accounts.
  • A balance only changes inside one database transaction, together with its ledger entries. A payout either reserves the money or is refused. Two payouts can’t both spend the same balance.
  • Your API key only reaches your project’s accounts. Another project’s account ID returns 404, so its balance can’t even be read.
  • Every day Rift checks that the total of all account balances is covered by the real balance at the payment partner, and that every ledger entry balances. If anything is off, a person is alerted before the next payout window.

Available and held

Section titled “Available and held”

Each account shows two numbers:

  • available: what you can pay out right now.
  • held: money reserved for payouts that are on their way. When a payout completes, the held amount leaves the account. When it fails, the held amount goes back to available. Either way it happens exactly once.

Authentication

Section titled “Authentication”

Call these endpoints from your server with your platform key, the same key as the Platform API, in one header:

(Authorization: Bearer psk_live_… works too.) Every POST that moves money also needs an Idempotency-Key: any unique string, such as a UUID. If a request times out, send it again with the same key. You get the original result back, with the header Idempotent-Replayed: true, and the money moves only once. If you reuse a key for a different request, you get 409 IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED.

The dashboard calls the same endpoints with your signed-in session.

Fees

Section titled “Fees”

Rift charges 0.99% on each collection and each payout, unless your agreement says otherwise. Your rate is on every account as feeBps (99 = 0.99%). The fee is shown in every response before and after the money moves:

  • Collection: the payer pays amount. Your account receives net = amount − fee.
  • Payout: the recipient gets exactly amount. Your account pays total = amount + fee.

Use the preview endpoints to show the fee (and the recipient’s registered name) before you confirm. They don’t move any money.

Open an account

Section titled “Open an account”

Opening the same currency again returns the existing account (200). Amounts are decimal strings. KES, TZS, UGX and MWK amounts are whole numbers; GHS allows two decimals.

Collect from mobile money

Section titled “Collect from mobile money”

The payer gets a prompt on their phone. The transaction stays pending until they approve it.

When the payer approves, the status becomes completed and net is added to available. If they decline or the prompt times out, the status becomes failed and nothing moves.

Kenyan collections need Rift’s collateral at the payment partner. While it’s being topped up, KES collections return 503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE. Nothing is created; try again later.

Pay out

Section titled “Pay out”

The response is the transaction with status: "held", fee and total. Recipient fields by type:

type Fields Currencies
mobile phone, network All
bank accountNumber, bankCode (from GET /v1/accounts/banks?currency=KES) KES
till till KES
paybill paybill, accountNumber KES

If available doesn’t cover amount + fee, you get 402 INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS and nothing is reserved. If Rift can’t pay out in that currency right now, you get 503 SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE and nothing is reserved.

When the outcome is unclear

Section titled “When the outcome is unclear”

If the payment network doesn’t answer clearly (for example, a timeout), Rift does not send the payout again. The transaction stays held while a person confirms with the network, then it becomes completed or failed. You never pay twice, and the reserved money is never lost.

Statuses

Section titled “Statuses”
Status Meaning
pending Collection started; waiting for the payer.
held Payout money is reserved and the payout is on its way.
completed Done. Collections: net is in available. Payouts: the recipient was paid.
failed Didn’t happen. Payouts: the held amount is back in available. failureReason says why.
reversed Reserved for corrections made by Rift.

Webhooks

Section titled “Webhooks”

Subscribe your webhook endpoint to these events. data is the transaction object shown above.

Event When
account.collection.completed The money arrived (net added to available).
account.collection.failed The payer declined, or the prompt timed out.
account.disbursement.completed The recipient was paid.
account.disbursement.failed The payout failed; the held amount is back in available.

Each event is sent once per transaction, even when Rift learns the result from more than one source.

Transactions and statements

Section titled “Transactions and statements”

A list returns { "transactions": [...], "nextCursor": "…" | null }. A statement includes the opening balance, every completed movement with a running balance, and the closing balance. Money that is only held shows up once the payout completes.

Compliance

Section titled “Compliance”

You attest that your end users are KYC’d under your platform terms; Rift runs KYB on your business before accounts go live. Rift can freeze an account (status: "frozen"). A frozen account refuses new collections and payouts, but money already on its way still settles.
