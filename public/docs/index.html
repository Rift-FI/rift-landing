Wallets your customers own.

WalletOS gives every customer a Safe smart account owned only by their passkeys, with gas handled for them and payouts, onramp and a full ledger built in. Rift holds no key that can move their money. Neither do you.

01

Non-custodial by design

Section titled “Non-custodial by design”

Each customer’s wallet is a Safe owned only by their passkeys and verified on-chain. Server and partner calls can only ask: the customer approves with a passkey.

02

No gas for end users

Section titled “No gas for end users”

Sponsor gas for your customers, or let them pay it in USDC or USDT. Nobody needs a native token to send money.

03

Rails built in

Section titled “Rails built in”

Payouts to mobile money, bank, till and paybill. Onramp (Beta) across seven currencies. Recipient name lookups before money moves.

04

One ledger

Section titled “One ledger”

Every customer transaction in one ledger, with statements on demand and, with consent, data you can use for credit decisions.
