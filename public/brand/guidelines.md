# Rift visual identity

Rift connects capital to Africa's productive economy.

## Logo
Use the existing Rift symbol with its original proportions. The website pairs it with the word RIFT in Inter Semibold. Keep clear space of at least half the symbol's width on every side. Do not stretch, recolour or add effects to the symbol. The PNG supplied is the original mark; no vector original was available in this repository.

## Colour and typography
Midnight #192535 for primary type; Rift blue #007D96 for actions; Ice #EEF4F7 for section backgrounds. Use Inter Regular for body copy, Medium for section headings and Semibold for primary headlines. Use generous line spacing and readable body sizes.

## Components
Buttons use a 5px corner radius. Section layouts rely on space and rules rather than bordered cards. Icons use a 24px view box, a consistent 1.5px stroke and rounded caps. Use one icon colour per surface. Do not put them in decorative pastel badges.

## Writing
Use direct headlines and plain language. Omit decorative all-caps labels, status dots, unsupported metrics and guaranteed-return language. Keep individual investors, institutional investors and business borrowers distinct. FX liquidity is one part of the broader sector focus.

## Images
Original AI-generated imagery was created for this design using the built-in image generation tool. The sculpture is abstract brand artwork. Photography is illustrative and does not document portfolio companies, users or customers. Do not present it as testimonial or investment evidence.
