WHY THESE QUESTIONS

Two jobs. Qualify the lead, and collect the inputs needed to actually run the
netting model on their corridor, so the first reply can contain a number
instead of a meeting request.

The two audiences need different questions, so question 2 branches.

Keep it short. Every extra field costs completions, and the people worth
hearing from are busy.

==========================================================================

Build me a contact form titled "Tell us about your corridor".

Intro text: "A few questions so we can come back with something useful rather
than a meeting invite. Takes two minutes."

QUESTION 1
Type: Short answer
Label: Your name
Required: yes

QUESTION 2
Type: Short answer
Label: Work email
Required: yes
Validation: email

QUESTION 3
Type: Short answer
Label: Company
Required: yes

QUESTION 4
Type: Multiple choice (single select)
Label: Which describes you best?
Required: yes
Options:
  - We hold dollars and deploy them into African trade
  - We move money across African borders
  - Both
  - Something else
Logic: if "We hold dollars and deploy them into African trade" go to Q5.
       if "We move money across African borders" or "Both" go to Q8.
       if "Something else" go to Q13.

--- BRANCH A: liquidity providers, DFIs, banks ---

QUESTION 5
Type: Multiple choice (single select)
Label: What kind of institution?
Options:
  - Development finance institution
  - Commercial bank
  - Fund or asset manager
  - Family office or private investor
  - Other

QUESTION 6
Type: Multiple choice (single select)
Label: Roughly what size of dollar facility could you deploy into a corridor?
Options:
  - Under 1 million
  - 1 to 5 million
  - 5 to 25 million
  - 25 to 100 million
  - Over 100 million
  - Not sure yet

QUESTION 7
Type: Long answer
Label: What does your mandate allow, and what would you need to see before committing?
Then go to Q13.

--- BRANCH B: people moving money ---

QUESTION 8
Type: Multiple choice (single select)
Label: What do you do?
Options:
  - Remittance company or money transfer operator
  - Payment service provider
  - Bank
  - Exporter or importer
  - Other

QUESTION 9
Type: Short answer
Label: Which corridors? For example Kenya to Nigeria, or Tanzania to UAE.
Required: yes

QUESTION 10
Type: Multiple choice (single select)
Label: Roughly how much moves across them in a month?
Options:
  - Under 100,000
  - 100,000 to 1 million
  - 1 to 10 million
  - 10 to 50 million
  - Over 50 million

QUESTION 11
Type: Multiple choice (multi select)
Label: How do you fund the payout side today?
Options:
  - Our own working capital sitting in the destination
  - A credit line or overdraft
  - Pre-funding from partners
  - We buy stablecoins as we need them
  - Other

QUESTION 12
Type: Short answer
Label: What does moving a dollar across that corridor cost you today, all in? A rough percentage is fine.

--- EVERYONE ---

QUESTION 13
Type: Long answer
Label: Anything else we should know?
Required: no

Thank-you screen: "Thanks. We read every one of these. If you told us about a
corridor we will come back with what netting does to your funding
requirement, usually within a day or two."
